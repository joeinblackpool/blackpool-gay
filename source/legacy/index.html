About

Britain's boldest seaside, at its proudest

Blackpool has been a place of freedom, fun and self-expression for generations. Today the town's LGBTQ+ scene — centred on the Rainbow Quarter around Queen Street — is bigger and brighter than ever.

🎡

Why Blackpool?

For over a century Blackpool has been where people come to let their hair down. That spirit of acceptance makes it a natural home for a vibrant, welcoming LGBTQ+ community by the sea.

  • A year-round calendar of cabaret, drag and live music
  • Blackpool Pride — a weekend of celebration every summer
  • The iconic Tower, the Pleasure Beach and the Golden Mile
  • The Illuminations lighting up the promenade each autumn
